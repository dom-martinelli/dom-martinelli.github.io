question

Chronic stress leaves measurable marks: HPA-axis dysregulation, glucocorticoid-receptor methylation, faster epigenetic clocks. Which of those marks are documented to change how people respond to psychiatric drugs, and does the burden fall on the same populations that are thin in the trials?

planned
  • planned figure: a three-layer network running from stress-response genes (FKBP5, NR3C1, CRHR1) through the pathways they share with drug targets to SSRIs, SNRIs and ketamine, with each edge sourced to a paper or database record.
  • reading list first: McEwen's allostatic-load model, the FKBP5 × early-life-stress work, and the antidepressant-response studies that use methylation as a moderator.
calls made
  • literature-first. every edge in the network has to point to an established finding, not to a model's guess.
