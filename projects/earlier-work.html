what is in here
  • chemception-style CNN on molecule images, retargeted to a hepatotoxicity endpoint (2022). the image encoder and inception scaffold come from a published tutorial; the dataset, the endpoint and the GPU port are mine.
  • molecular dynamics of two peptides in GROMACS with CHARMM36, energy minimisation through NVT and NPT to a 10 ns production run (2022). the analysis notebook is adapted from a public teaching lab; the trajectories are my own runs.
  • a pegRNA design tool for prime editing, interactive at the command line, with its own validation messages for spacer, PBS and RTT lengths (2024). written from scratch.
  • clinical NLP on national survey data: NAMCS visit records wrangled against hand-built ICD and RxNorm lookup tables, plus a parser for the n2c2 de-identification ground truth (2024).
  • multi-omics cohort analyses across proteomics and metabolomics studies (2024 to 2025), the work behind the multi-omics line on my resume.
  • writing came first: two ChEMBL and Keras write-ups on inhibiting the hepatitis C polyprotein (2021), the oldest machine-learning-on-chemistry thing I have.
