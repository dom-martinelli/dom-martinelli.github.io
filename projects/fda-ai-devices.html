question

What does the FDA's public list of AI-enabled devices record, and what does it leave out about whether those devices help patients?

calls made
  • per-device table and aggregate evidence findings kept in separate sections. the published studies report across the whole list, so no single device can carry one of those values.
  • framed as an evidence and transparency question, not as 510(k) clearance being wrongdoing. the pathway is legal and often appropriate.
  • missing fields reported as not reported, not as not done. a study may exist and simply be absent from the public summary, which is itself the finding.
  • says plainly that the list undercounts. it is assembled from AI terminology in public summaries, so devices can be missing.
what broke
  • v1 was a fixed essay whose headline figures came from an older snapshot and secondary sources of different vintages.
  • the page's own copy read as machine-written on a later teardown: adversative title, hero stats, closing aphorism. rewritten against my style rules.
what i tried

v1 was a static evidence essay built on other people's summary figures. rebuilt as a filterable registry over the FDA's own downloaded list, so the per-device record and the aggregate evidence stay visibly separate.

figures made with matplotlib · site built from json by build.py
