question

If a benchmark's headline finding is that one number hides the weak slices, can a reader check that for themselves instead of taking my word for it?

calls made
  • the delta column, not the raw accuracy, is the thing the app is built around. a filtered score only means something next to the same model's overall score.
  • every claim in the paper should be re-derivable from the app by a reader who does not trust me. that is why the item browser shows the inclusion reason and the identifier provenance, not just the question.
  • the contribute tab deliberately does not write to the dataset. submissions enter a review queue and a maintainer reviews them by hand, which is the honest limit of one reviewer.
  • identifier columns are hidden by default and only source-grounded exact mappings get loaded. pending ontology candidates never reach the app.
what broke
  • the slice heatmap currently renders 'no items for that slice' on both axes even with no filter applied and all items selected. caught by stepping through the live Space rather than the local test harness.
not shown
  • the manuscript's external-comparison table against MedQA, MedMCQA and PubMedQA is not mirrored in the app
