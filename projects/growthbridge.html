question

What would it look like for a family to see and understand their child's growth record between visits?

calls made
  • flags computed from a z-score threshold at render time, not hand-written trend annotations per scenario. scripted copy cannot survive real record data.
  • read-only, no backend. this demonstrates the family-facing view; it is not a clinical product.
  • home-entered measurements stay in the family's own browser storage. there is no backend, and the data never has to leave the device.
  • falls back to a mock scenario when the SMART launch fails, so the app opens from a plain link without a launch context.
what broke
  • the Turner teaching case had been narrated as a percentile drop the z-score rule would never flag. the mock percentiles had to move to values the computed rule actually catches.
  • Pages deployment needed explicit job permissions and newer action versions before it worked.
  • Epic's exact redirect matching makes a project-subpath URL a trap: site root and index.html are not the same registered value.
what i tried

scripted per-scenario trend insights went away once flags were computed from the threshold, since a hand-written percentile-drop story cannot follow real record data.

not shown
  • percentiles are not from a validated engine, the midparental formula has no documented source yet, and it has never been run against a real Epic client
