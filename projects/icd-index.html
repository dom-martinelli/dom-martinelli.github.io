question

When a model scores well on a medical benchmark, which diseases was it actually tested on, and which ones never come up?

what came out
  • 14,088 unique items across nine public benchmarks; 5,000 of them (HealthBench) have no answer key, and are kept as coverage rather than scored.
  • names are never coded by the model itself. each condition goes through the official ICD-10-CM Alphabetic Index first, then fuzzy and embedding matches, and anything unmatched stays visible rather than dropping out of the counts.
calls made
  • condition roles, not just mentions: tested, context, ruled out, or appearing only in a wrong option. a disease named in a distractor is not a disease the benchmark tests.
  • question text is withheld wherever a dataset's licence does not clearly allow redistribution; the explorer shows identifiers and derived labels instead.
  • everything runs on local models, so the whole index cost nothing in API fees and can be rerun by anyone.
not shown
  • validation numbers are held for the paper in progress with Chris and Joon
