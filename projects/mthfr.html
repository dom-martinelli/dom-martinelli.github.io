MTHFR: why the trials couldn't see it

a gene's effect that depends on diet, and trials run where the diet erased it

status
planned
question

The MTHFR 677TT genotype raises homocysteine, but only where people lack folate. If most homocysteine-lowering trials ran in countries that fortify flour with folic acid, were they ever able to detect an effect?

planned
  • a world map with TT genotype frequency as fill and mandatory folic-acid fortification as hatching, with the homocysteine-lowering trial sites on top: the trials sit where the gene matters least.
  • an effect surface: population folate status × genotype against the homocysteine difference, flat in fortified regions and rising where folate is low (Holmes: 3.12 vs 0.13 µmol/L).
  • a short note on the gap between that evidence and the direct-to-consumer MTHFR testing market.
