Do patients' ideas reach the research?

Long COVID, where patients named and studied the illness before most researchers did

tools

data
Patient-Led Research Collaborative survey reports (2020 onward) · PubMed and iCite citation data · a rule-based comparison pool of papers that do not engage patient-led work
status
in progress
question

Long COVID patients ran their own surveys and named symptoms like post-exertional malaise before most of the literature did. Do the ideas patients introduced show up in the research that engages their work, and how long do they take to arrive?

planned
  • dating each symptom in the patient-led reports against the first PubMed paper to describe it.
  • comparing how the papers that cite patient-led work frame those symptoms against a matched comparison pool.
  • ME/CFS as the historical contrast: graded exercise versus pacing, and the 2021 NICE guideline reversal.
