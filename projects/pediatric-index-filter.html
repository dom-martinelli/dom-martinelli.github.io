Who is the patient? A pediatric index-patient filter

keeping the medical questions whose primary clinical subject is a child, and dropping the ones that only mention one

status
in progress
Where it is used: the Growth-MedQA benchmark
question

A keyword sweep for child, infant and adolescent returns far more pediatric questions than are really pediatric. Can the primary clinical subject be identified instead of the vocabulary?

calls made
  • the rule is one sentence and it is the whole method: do not classify a question as pediatric just because it contains child terms. identify the primary clinical subject and the target of the question.
  • the shipped filter is deterministic, dependency-light and needs no API keys. the expensive classifier earned its place by handling the cases the deterministic pass cannot, not by replacing it.
  • the classifier was improved by rewriting the instructions around primary subject and question target. adding few-shot examples was the wrong lever: the failure mode was keyword surface matching, not a shortage of examples.
  • every row goes through the same measurement process in one frozen labeling run, rather than routing only prefilter-positive rows to the stronger classifier.
what broke
  • agreement with PedMedQA was nearly reported as validated accuracy before I checked its own labels and found errors in them, including a 19-year-old labeled as an infant. it is a cross-reference and a lower bound, and the docs now say so.
  • the neonate gap was found by reading recall by age band rather than in aggregate. the aggregate number looked fine.
what i tried

a sandbox version turned the neonatal override on by default and lifted overall recall to 95.6%. it is not the default because it trades specificity back, so it ships as a flag with the numbers for both settings.

not shown
  • the 269-item calibration labels are accepted model labels with a blinded audit, not independent clinician gold. that distinction is in the repo and it matters.
