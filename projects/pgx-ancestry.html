question

For which drugs does a CPIC dosing recommendation hinge on an allele whose frequency differs most between populations, and who was in the trials those drugs were approved on?

planned
  • planned figure: a gene–drug network where each drug node is sized by how far the actionable-phenotype frequency spreads across biogeographic groups. CYP2C19 and clopidogrel, HLA-B*15:02 and carbamazepine, and CYP2D6 and codeine are the known anchors.
  • second view: approval-trial demographics for the same drugs, from FDA Drug Trials Snapshots, set against those frequencies.
calls made
  • recommendations come from CPIC, the consortium guidelines clinicians actually dose from, rather than from single association studies.
