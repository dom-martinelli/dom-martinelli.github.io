question

When PharmGKB says a gene changes how a drug works, which clinical trials is that claim resting on, and whose bodies were in them?

what came out
  • 8,027 PharmGKB 'associated' relationship–paper links, matched to ClinicalTrials.gov references, give 254 trials behind the gene–drug claims, 202 with full records.
  • 45 of those trials, supporting 51 papers, back relationships whose only evidence comes from outside the US. Germany has the most non-US sites (370).
  • 42 of the trials were withdrawn, and the withdrawal reasons are tallied in the notebook.
calls made
  • the link runs PharmGKB relationship → cited paper → trial registry number, so a trial only counts if a paper behind a gene–drug claim names it.
  • site country is the unit, not sponsor country. a claim tested only in German clinics is a claim about German patients.
what broke
  • a missingness pass reported sex, race and ethnicity missing in every one of the 202 trials, with only 3 study–gene matches. that is almost certainly a join error, not a finding, and it is left off until it is rechecked.
not shown
  • the three case studies planned on top of this (zolpidem sex-based dosing, carbamazepine with HLA-B*15:02, warfarin genotype dosing)
