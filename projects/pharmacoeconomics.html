question

Lalani and colleagues estimated Medicare could have saved $3.6 billion of $9.6 billion on 89 generics by paying cost-plus prices. Where does that overpayment land, county by county?

  • a county choropleth of estimated overpayment per beneficiary, pairing the geography-level Part D file with cost-plus prices for the same generics.
  • a ridgeline of estimated R&D cost per approved drug by therapeutic area, from Wouters, McKee and Luyten (JAMA 2020), as the context for what a price is supposed to recover.
