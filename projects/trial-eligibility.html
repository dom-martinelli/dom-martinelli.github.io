question

If you apply a typical psychiatric trial's exclusion criteria to everyone in the country who has the disorder, how many real patients would have been allowed in?

planned
  • eligibility funnels drawn as an alluvial: everyone with the disorder enters on the left, each exclusion (comorbid substance use, suicidality, other psychiatric illness, medications) peels off a stream, and the thin trickle on the right is who a trial could enrol. small multiples for depression, bipolar disorder, panic and alcohol use disorder.
  • the anchor numbers are published: Zimmerman found most outpatients with depression would fail standard antidepressant-trial criteria, and Hoertel's group found 96.9% of treatment-seekers with alcohol dependence and a mood or anxiety disorder would be ineligible.
  • tie-in from the advocacy-and-accrual work: 52% of depression trials already exclude people with another psychiatric illness.
