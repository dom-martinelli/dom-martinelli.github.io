Caveats

  1. device table is the FDA's per-device record. evidence table is whole-list findings from the studies. kept separate; no evidence value is attached to any single device.
  2. FDA calls its own list non-comprehensive. built from AI-related terms in public summaries, so it undercounts.
  3. "not reported" is not "not done." evidence can exist and be missing from the public summary.
  4. 510(k) is legal and often sensible. this is about evidence and transparency, not wrongdoing.
  5. built from public data. not affiliated with or endorsed by the FDA.
