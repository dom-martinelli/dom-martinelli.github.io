Where Stroke Belt patients go for a hospital stay

Each arc is Medicare inpatient stays from a patient's home county to the county of the hospital that treated them, across eight Stroke Belt states. Arcs shade from light at home to deep at the hospital. Dots are home counties, sized by how many stays leave.
